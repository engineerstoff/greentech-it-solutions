Automation

Where small businesses can start with AI automation

Start with repetitive work that already has clear inputs and outputs.

The best first automation is usually the repetitive task that wastes time every week. Map the process first, choose one measurable workflow, and keep approvals and sensitive decisions under human control.

Networking

Signs your business network needs a redesign

Repeated Wi-Fi problems often point to the architecture underneath.

Shared passwords, random extenders, no separation between staff and guests, and no visibility into bandwidth usage are signs the network needs a planned redesign rather than another quick patch.

Web Strategy

What a Ghanaian SME website should do beyond looking good

A useful site should help people understand, trust and contact the business.

Make the offer clear, use accurate contact details, show real evidence as it becomes available, make WhatsApp and enquiry actions easy, and design for mobile first.
